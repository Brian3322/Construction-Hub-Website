About Us

Construction Hub is a premier peer-to-peer marketplace designed to bridge the gap between supply and demand in the building industry. We empower individuals and SMEs by providing a platform to trade construction materials—from cement bags to steel bars—while offering companies a specialized space to list heavy machinery for daily rental.

Our mission focuses on trust, accessibility, and efficiency. Whether you are a contractor looking for a crane or a DIYer needing surplus bricks, we make industrial procurement seamless and cost-effective.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I buy construction materials?

Simply browse the product section, click on "View Details," and contact the seller directly through our secure platform messaging.

How does equipment rental work?

Rentals are charged on a daily basis. You can select your dates and book the machinery; the company will then confirm availability and delivery.

Can companies list their products for free?

Yes, we offer a basic free listing tier for small companies and individuals to post their surplus materials.

Is delivery available?

Delivery depends on the seller or rental provider. Many listings offer delivery for an additional fee calculated at checkout.

Machinery Overview

The JCB 940 RTFL is a high-capacity all-terrain forklift engineered for the most demanding construction sites. Featuring a high-torque Dieselmax engine and a 4-wheel drive system, it excels in moving heavy palletized loads across mud, gravel, and uneven surfaces. Ideal for large-scale logistics, brick-laying support, and remote site infrastructure projects.
